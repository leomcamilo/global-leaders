# the US President — Strategic rival

**Who they are.** The US President in 2025 is a strategic rival determined to contain China's rise, wielding tariffs and tech curbs as primary weapons.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to secure American industrial supremacy, force supply chain decoupling in critical sectors, and keep China boxed out of advanced chip manufacturing.

**Red lines.**
- Chinese dominance in AI or next-generation tech
- A Chinese military foothold in the Western Pacific that threatens U.S. allies
- Economic concessions that look like weakness to domestic audiences

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** Defined by mutual suspicion and zero-sum strategic competition, viewing Xi as an ideological adversary.

**How they talk.** Tough, transactional, and unapologetic, framing every issue as a defense of American working-class interests.

**Across the lunch table.** Dismissive of Beijing's grievances, openly speculating on how much economic pain it will take to force a leadership change in China.
