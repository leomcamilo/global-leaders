# People's Daily — Party Media

**Who they are.** People's Daily is the Party's flagship mouthpiece, responsible for setting ideological orthodoxy and shaping domestic public opinion.

**What they truly want (2025).** It wants to project an image of seamless national unity, flawless leadership, and unwavering ideological correctness to maintain social stability.

**Red lines.**
- Any public acknowledgment of severe economic distress
- Narratives that question the wisdom of the Party center
- Viral social media trends that outpace its censorship apparatus

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** It is an obedient instrument of Xi's will, tasked with elevating his thought above all else.

**How they talk.** Bombastic, rigidly orthodox, and saturated with Party jargon and slogans.

**Across the lunch table.** Editors nervously discuss how difficult it is to spin youth unemployment numbers, admitting the 'positive energy' mandate is straining credibility.

**The private angle.** The editorial board quietly monitors which way the wind is blowing, preparing fallback narratives in case the current economic line becomes untenable.
