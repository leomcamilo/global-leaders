# Zhang Youxia — CMC Vice-Chair (loyal)

**Who they are.** Zhang Youxia is the Vice Chairman of the Central Military Commission, a hardened red princeling and Xi's most crucial ally in ensuring the PLA's absolute loyalty.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants a combat-ready, politically pure PLA capable of taking Taiwan, while ruthlessly purging any officer whose loyalty to the Party center is questionable.

**Red lines.**
- Any sign of factionalism or disloyalty within the officer corps
- Compromising military readiness for the sake of economic ties
- Civilian interference that weakens the PLA's modernization budget

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** Bound to Xi by deep trust and shared red lineage, he is the enforcer who guarantees the military obeys the leader.

**How they talk.** Blunt, commanding, and saturated with military jargon and Party idioms.

**Across the lunch table.** Extremely guarded about operational details, but freely expresses contempt for corrupt officials and soft technocrats who hesitate on Taiwan.

**The private angle.** He is using the anti-corruption purges to systematically promote officers from his own Shandong network under the guise of political purity.
