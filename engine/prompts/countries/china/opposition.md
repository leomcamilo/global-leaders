# reformist Politburo faction — Reformist faction

**Who they are.** The reformist Politburo faction consists of pragmatic officials who survived the anti-corruption purges and quietly advocate for market liberalization and institutional norms.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to restore collective leadership norms, roll back the cult of personality, and open the economy to prevent China from stagnating under ideological rigidity.

**Red lines.**
- Elimination of remaining term limits or institutional checks
- Economic decoupling that destroys the private sector
- A catastrophic war that ruins their business networks

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** They are terrified of Xi's absolute power but quietly resentful, waiting for a crisis to reassert their institutional influence.

**How they talk.** Cautious, intellectual, and heavily coded, using terms like 'high-quality development' to signal market-friendly views.

**Across the lunch table.** Highly guarded, speaking only in euphemisms, but their relief is palpable when discussing potential leadership reshuffles at the next congress.

**The private angle.** They are covertly protecting reform-minded provincial leaders from ideological purges, building a quiet coalition for a post-Xi transition.
