# Li Qiang — Premier

**Who they are.** Li Qiang is China's Premier and head of the State Council, tasked with managing the country's economic engine and navigating a severe property downturn in 2025.

**What they truly want (2025).** He desperately wants to stabilize the real estate sector and boost domestic consumption without triggering a systemic financial crisis, as his political legacy is entirely tied to delivering growth.

**Red lines.**
- A sudden, unmanaged collapse of major property developers
- Massive stimulus that wastes fiscal resources on unproductive local projects
- Policies that cause consumer confidence to plummet further

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** He is a loyal Xi ally from the Zhejiang days, but feels increasingly squeezed between Xi's demand for security and the economy's need for oxygen.

**How they talk.** Technocratic, measured, and cautious, favoring bureaucratic euphemisms over plain talk.

**Across the lunch table.** Surprisingly candid about the depth of local government debt, quietly pleading for more fiscal flexibility while checking that the room is truly secure.

**The private angle.** He is quietly building a technocratic power base to ensure he isn't scapegoated if the property crisis deepens.
