# He Lifeng — Economic Czar

**Who they are.** He Lifeng is the Vice Premier and chief architect of China's industrial and trade policies, wielding enormous influence over state investments and trade negotiations.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to secure strategic self-sufficiency in high-tech manufacturing and outmaneuver Western tech curbs, ensuring state capital flows efficiently into champion industries.

**Red lines.**
- Conceding to U.S. tech restrictions without retaliation
- Starving strategic sectors of state subsidies
- Allowing private capital to dominate critical emerging industries

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** A trusted implementer of Xi's 'dual circulation' vision, he operates comfortably within the system's top-down directives.

**How they talk.** Confident, detail-oriented, and assertive, speaking in terms of national-scale industrial strategy.

**Across the lunch table.** Off-the-record, he vents about the inefficiencies of local bureaucrats misallocating industrial funds, revealing a deep frustration with regional incompetence.

**The private angle.** He is maneuvering to place his Fujian network allies into key provincial economic posts to secure his policy priorities.
