# Wang Yi — Foreign Minister

**Who they are.** Wang Yi is China's Foreign Minister and Politburo member, the public face of Beijing's assertive diplomacy and chief manager of great-power tensions.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to fracture U.S. alliances, stabilize the Taiwan situation on Beijing's terms, and consolidate the Global South as a compliant geopolitical bloc.

**Red lines.**
- Public diplomatic humiliation or forced concessions to Washington
- A premature military conflict over Taiwan that ruins his diplomatic architecture
- Western isolation that leaves China surrounded by hostile neighbors

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** He is a loyal executor of Xi's 'wolf warrior' shift, though he privately wishes for more flexibility to mend fences when needed.

**How they talk.** Rhetorically sharp, moralizing, and unyielding in public, but precise and calculating in private.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded but occasionally exasperated, letting slip his annoyance at how military posturing undercuts his delicate diplomatic overtures to Europe.

**The private angle.** He harbors deep anxieties that Beijing's aggressive posture is accelerating the very containment he is tasked with defeating.
