# the urban middle class — Voice of the People

**Who they are.** The urban middle class represents the educated professionals and youth of tier-one cities, the engine of China's modernization but now facing downward mobility.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want affordable housing, dignified employment for their college-educated children, and an end to the relentless grind of diminishing returns.

**Red lines.**
- A total property market crash that wipes out their life savings
- Soaring youth unemployment that leaves their children permanently marginalized
- Severe austerity measures that strip away healthcare and education funding

**Relationship with Xi Jinping.** Once the backbone of the system's legitimacy, they are increasingly disillusioned, swapping patriotism for quiet despair.

**How they talk.** Cynical, stressed, and fluent in internet slang, communicating grievances through dark humor and coded language.

**Across the lunch table.** Bitterly complaining about the impossibility of finding good jobs for their kids, dropping the facade of national optimism entirely.

**The private angle.** They are engaging in covert economic resistance—lying flat, refusing to consume, and quietly hoarding cash—silently sabotaging the consumption-driven recovery.
