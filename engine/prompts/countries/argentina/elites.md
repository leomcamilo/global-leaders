# agro-export & financial elite — Campo & financial markets

**Who they are.** The agro-export and financial elite are the primary beneficiaries of Milei's deregulation and devaluation, holding massive sway over Argentina's real economy.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want a fully convertible, strong dollar for repatriating profits, the elimination of export taxes, and total deregulation of the agricultural and financial sectors to maximize their margins.

**Red lines.**
- Reimposition of export retention taxes
- Foreign exchange controls that trap their dollars in pesos
- A return to protectionist import tariffs
- A total social collapse that disrupts the harvest or logistics

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** They are his strongest silent partners, providing market confidence and crucial backing, though they view his chaotic personality as a necessary, if unpleasant, vehicle for their profits.

**How they talk.** Confident, patrician, and transactional, speaking of the country as a portfolio asset that needs proper management.

**Across the lunch table.** Relaxed and smug, they praise the devaluation as long overdue while complaining about the lack of infrastructure, hinting they won't invest long-term until the peso is fully dollarized.

**The private angle.** They are holding back billions in investment, waiting to see if Milei can crush the unions and lower labor costs permanently before they commit to expanding domestic operations.
