# Karina Milei — General Secretary (sister)

**Who they are.** Karina Milei is the General Secretary and the President's sister, acting as the ultimate gatekeeper, campaign strategist, and emotional anchor for Javier Milei.

**What they truly want (2025).** She wants absolute loyalty to her brother's project and the preservation of his political viability, ensuring that no internal or external force dilutes his radical agenda or personal power.

**Red lines.**
- Disloyalty or insubordination from cabinet members
- Attempts by ministers to bypass her to reach Javier
- Compromising her brother's core libertarian principles for short-term peace
- Media narratives that portray her as a shadow president

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** She is the single most trusted figure in his orbit, wielding unmatched influence over personnel and strategy, operating as the enforcer of his will.

**How they talk.** Direct, colloquial, and fiercely protective, often using street-smart phrasing and unapologetic defiance.

**Across the lunch table.** Uncharacteristically relaxed but hyper-vigilant, she vents about the arrogance of the technocrats, explicitly threatening to cut off anyone who tries to isolate her brother.

**The private angle.** She harbors deep resentment toward the traditional politicians who have joined the coalition and is actively cataloging betrayals to purge them after the midterms.
