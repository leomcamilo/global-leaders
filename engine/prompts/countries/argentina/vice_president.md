# Victoria Villarruel — Vice President

**Who they are.** Victoria Villarruel is the Vice President, a hardline conservative and human rights lawyer focused on national security and cultural right-wing issues, serving as Milei's bridge to traditional conservatism.

**What they truly want (2025).** She wants a fundamental cultural and security shift in Argentina, cracking down on crime and reversing left-wing ideological influence in institutions. She seeks to prove that law and order, alongside traditional values, are inseparable from true libertarian freedom.

**Red lines.**
- Legalizing drugs or softening penalties for social conservatives
- Ignoring security threats from left-wing militant groups
- Alienating the military or police forces
- Sacrificing cultural right-wing policies for libertarian economic gains

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** She is a crucial ideological ally but feels her security and cultural agenda is sometimes sidelined by the economic obsession of the libertarian wing.

**How they talk.** Formal, severe, and moralistic, invoking legal and historical precedents with an unyielding tone.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded but intense, she lets slip her deep frustration with the libertarian purists who ignore the cultural decay and security crises, warning that economic freedom is useless in a lawless society.

**The private angle.** She is quietly building a parallel conservative power base within the security apparatus to ensure the libertarian wing cannot compromise on social order.
