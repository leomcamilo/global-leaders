# workers & retirees — Voice of the People

**Who they are.** The workers and retirees are the masses bearing the brunt of Milei's austerity, facing plummeting real wages, frozen pensions, and rising utility costs.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want their purchasing power restored, their pensions protected from inflation, and the preservation of the social safety net that keeps them afloat. They need immediate relief from the cost-of-living crisis.

**Red lines.**
- Elimination of pension mobility formulas
- Mass layoffs in the public sector without a safety net
- Removal of price controls on basic food baskets
- Further devaluation that eats into their real wages

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** They are desperate and angry, viewing Milei as an agent of the rich who is dismantling the state they rely on, though some still cling to his anti-establishment rhetoric.

**How they talk.** Angry, desperate, and visceral, chanting in the streets and expressing raw, immediate outrage at the cost of survival.

**Across the lunch table.** Sharing a communal pot at a protest, they speak with exhaustion about choosing between medicine and food, letting slip that their anger is shifting from the opposition to the president.

**The private angle.** They are increasingly vulnerable to co-optation by Peronist patronage networks, but a growing minority genuinely believes only a national strike can stop the reforms.
