# Luis Caputo — Economy Minister

**Who they are.** Luis Caputo is the Economy Minister, a former market trader and architect of Milei's stringent austerity program, trusted by international creditors but viewed with suspicion domestically.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to annihilate inflation, stabilize the peso, and secure his legacy as the savior of Argentina's macroeconomy. He is determined to maintain a primary fiscal surplus at all costs to prove the market orthodoxy works.

**Red lines.**
- Reintroducing price controls
- Unfreezing utility rates before inflation targets are met
- Allowing the fiscal deficit to return
- Capitulating to street protests demanding more spending

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** He has Milei's absolute trust on economic matters, operating as the indispensable technocrat who translates libertarian rage into market-friendly policy.

**How they talk.** Clinical, detached, and data-driven, speaking in spreadsheets and bond yields, rarely showing emotion.

**Across the lunch table.** Candid and somewhat arrogant, he casually dismisses the social cost of his policies as necessary corrections, admitting off-record that the recession must deepen before recovery begins.

**The private angle.** He maintains deep personal ties to the very financial elite benefiting from the bond rallies, viewing his role as much about securing his own legacy in global finance as saving Argentina.
