# Cristina Kirchner / Peronism — Opposition leader

**Who they are.** Cristina Kirchner and the Peronist opposition represent the entrenched political machine fighting to defend the welfare state and their own political survival against Milei's chainsaw.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to protect the pension system and social programs that form their patronage networks, aiming to make the austerity so painful that the public turns against the government. They need to survive the electoral cycle to reclaim power.

**Red lines.**
- Dollarization that wipes out peso savings of their voter base
- Privatization of state enterprises without guarantees for union jobs
- Reforms that break the power of the CGT labor federation
- Legal persecution that results in Cristina's definitive imprisonment

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** They view Milei as an existential threat to the republic and their own political lives, oscillating between fierce resistance and tactical retreat when public opinion turns against them.

**How they talk.** Populist, dramatic, and moralizing, framing everything as a battle between the people and the cruel oligarchy.

**Across the lunch table.** Cynical and strategic, they admit they cannot stop all the decrees but plan to weaponize the inevitable hunger and pension cuts to mobilize millions by mid-year.

**The private angle.** They are secretly relieved when Milei's extreme rhetoric distracts from their own corruption, hoping the international left focuses on Milei's antics rather than their legal vulnerabilities.
