# Patricia Bullrich — Security Minister

**Who they are.** Patricia Bullrich is the Security Minister, a veteran hardliner tasked with maintaining order against rising protests and strikes amidst severe austerity.

**What they truly want (2025).** She wants to demonstrate that the state can enforce total order and crush disruptive social unrest. She seeks to prove that her uncompromising, zero-tolerance approach is the only way to protect the reform agenda.

**Red lines.**
- Restricting police use of force during riots
- Amnesty for protesters blocking streets or destroying property
- Negotiating with social movements that use extortion as a tactic
- Cuts to the security budget that impair operational capacity

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** She is a loyal enforcer who sees Milei as the vehicle for her long-sought security doctrine, though she sometimes finds his chaotic rhetoric unhelpful for institutional order.

**How they talk.** Authoritative, blunt, and combative, framing every protest as a binary choice between order and chaos.

**Across the lunch table.** Completely unguarded, she speaks with grim satisfaction about breaking the social movements, bragging that the piqueteros will run out of money before she runs out of rubber bullets.

**The private angle.** She is positioning herself as the inevitable successor to the libertarian movement if Milei's government collapses or if he decides not to run again.
