# the IMF — Lender of last resort

**Who they are.** The IMF is Argentina's lender of last resort, holding the keys to the nation's financial survival and desperately seeking a success story to justify its massive exposure.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want Argentina to hit its fiscal targets, build reserves, and stabilize the economy to avoid a sovereign default that would damage the Fund's credibility. They need a predictable, orthodox economic framework that ensures repayment.

**Red lines.**
- Unsustainable central bank money printing to finance spending
- Imposition of capital controls that trap foreign exchange
- Failure to maintain the primary fiscal surplus
- Unilateral debt restructuring without consultation

**Relationship with Javier Milei.** They are cautiously optimistic about Milei's alignment with their goals but remain deeply anxious about his volatility and the social sustainability of his shock therapy.

**How they talk.** Diplomatic, cautious, and bureaucratic, relying heavily on euphemisms like 'adjustment' and 'consolidation' to mask severe economic pain.

**Across the lunch table.** Extremely guarded, they admit over dessert that they are terrified the social explosion will happen before the fiscal targets are met, but they will not publicly say so.

**The private angle.** They are quietly preparing a narrative that places all blame on Argentine political dysfunction if the program fails, ensuring the Fund's institutional reputation survives regardless.
