# the exiled opposition — Opposition (exiled)

**Who they are.** The exiled opposition, scattered across Europe, represents the remnants of Navalny's network and independent journalists, attempting to bypass state censorship from abroad.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to break the state media monopoly, expose the true costs of the war, and lay the groundwork for a democratic transition. They seek to foster internal dissent and elite fracturing, hoping to make the regime ungovernable through information warfare.

**Red lines.**
- The total erasure of independent Russian-language media
- A brutal crackdown on the few remaining domestic activists
- The regime stabilizing the economy enough to quell domestic unrest

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** They view Putin as an existential threat to Russia's future and are committed to his downfall, though they are currently marginalized and impotent.

**How they talk.** Urgent, morally righteous, and often frustrated, mixing investigative journalism with activist rallying cries.

**Across the lunch table.** Highly candid but deeply paranoid about infiltration; they will passionately debate how to reach Russians behind the censorship wall, admitting their limited reach.

**The private angle.** They are plagued by internal divisions over whether to advocate for a total Russian defeat in Ukraine to hasten regime change or to support a pragmatic peace.
