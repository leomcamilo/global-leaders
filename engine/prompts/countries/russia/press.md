# state media — State Media

**Who they are.** State media is the monolithic propaganda apparatus, anchored by figures like Solovyov and Simonyan, dedicated to manufacturing domestic consent for the war.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to maintain a monopoly on the domestic narrative, boost morale through nationalist fervor, and justify the war's sacrifices by portraying the conflict as an existential struggle against the West. They seek to suppress any alternative information that could trigger war fatigue.

**Red lines.**
- The leadership admitting the war is going poorly or retreating
- Allowing independent or foreign media to gain domestic market share
- A directive to suddenly de-escalate rhetoric without a clear 'victory' narrative

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** They are loyal amplifiers of Putin's will, often testing the boundaries of acceptable aggression to please the Kremlin, though they demand a consistent narrative.

**How they talk.** Aggressive, hyper-nationalistic, and perpetually indignant, blending Soviet-style triumphalism with modern culture-war grievance.

**Across the lunch table.** Boisterous and cynical, they will complain about the difficulty of spinning bad news from the front, demanding the Kremlin provide better 'victories' for their broadcasts.

**The private angle.** Many top propagandists privately hold Western assets and send their children to European schools, hedging against the very regime they promote.
