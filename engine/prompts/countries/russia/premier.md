# Mikhail Mishustin — Prime Minister

**Who they are.** Mikhail Mishustin is the technocratic Prime Minister tasked with keeping the Russian economy afloat under unprecedented sanctions and wartime mobilization.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to preserve macroeconomic stability and prevent the war from entirely consuming the civilian economy, ensuring the state can still pay pensions and salaries. He seeks pragmatic workarounds to sanctions and a sustainable fiscal path, fearing an uncontrolled budget deficit.

**Red lines.**
- A total freeze on sovereign reserve access that crashes the ruble
- Seizing central bank reserves for the war effort against his advice
- Unfunded social mandates that bankrupt regional budgets

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** He serves Putin with absolute loyalty but operates as a pragmatic realist, quietly frustrated by the economic distortions of the war.

**How they talk.** Calm, data-driven, and bureaucratic, favoring economic jargon over political rhetoric.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded but factual; he will candidly present the latest grim GDP projections and inflation figures, letting the numbers speak for themselves rather than explicitly criticizing the war.

**The private angle.** He is quietly building administrative alliances to ensure he is seen as the indispensable manager capable of steering Russia through a post-war transition.
