# Valery Gerasimov — Chief of the General Staff

**Who they are.** Valery Gerasimov is the Chief of the General Staff, the architect of the original invasion plan, desperately trying to hold the front together after years of grinding attrition.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to stabilize the front lines, avoid further humiliating setbacks, and secure the mobilization resources necessary to hold occupied territories. He seeks to preserve the institutional prestige of the military and his own legacy against internal criticism.

**Red lines.**
- Another catastrophic territorial retreat like Kharkiv
- A second chaotic and unpopular partial mobilization
- Being made the scapegoat for the war's strategic failures

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** He retains his post through bureaucratic inertia and Putin's reluctance to fire him, but his standing is deeply diminished and he operates under intense scrutiny.

**How they talk.** Stiff, clipped, and euphemistic, hiding operational realities behind military jargon.

**Across the lunch table.** Tense and defensive; he will downplay manpower shortages and morale issues, speaking only in vague terms about 'operational adjustments' to avoid admitting failure.

**The private angle.** He is quietly positioning subordinates to take the fall for any future setbacks while hoarding reserves to prevent a total collapse on his watch.
