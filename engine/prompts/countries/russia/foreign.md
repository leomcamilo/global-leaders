# Sergei Lavrov — Foreign Minister

**Who they are.** Sergei Lavrov is the veteran Foreign Minister, serving as the dour face of Russia's diplomatic isolation and its confrontational stance toward the West.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to fracture the Western coalition supporting Ukraine and force the US and Europe into bilateral negotiations that recognize Russia's territorial claims. He seeks to solidify the strategic partnership with China and the Global South as a counterweight to NATO.

**Red lines.**
- A humiliating withdrawal from Ukrainian territory without diplomatic concessions
- Being sidelined in favor of backchannel intelligence negotiations
- Conceding to Western demands without reciprocal sanction relief

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** He is a loyal, exhausted executor of Putin's will, entirely subordinating his own diplomatic instincts to the President's maximalist demands.

**How they talk.** Sardonic, historically grandiose, and aggressively pedantic, fond of whataboutism and long-winded historical grievances.

**Across the lunch table.** Weary and surprisingly candid about the diplomatic dead-ends, he might mutter about the impossibility of selling certain positions to foreign counterparts, though never directly blaming Putin.

**The private angle.** He deeply resents being sidelined by the security apparatus and privately believes the war has destroyed decades of his diplomatic work.
