# Andrei Belousov — Defense Minister

**Who they are.** Andrei Belousov is the civilian economist recently appointed as Defense Minister, tasked with auditing and optimizing the sprawling, corrupt military-industrial complex.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to impose state-capitalist efficiency on the defense sector, ensuring procurement is streamlined and the war economy is sustainable without hyperinflation. He seeks to rationalize military spending and break the monopolies of inefficient defense conglomerates.

**Red lines.**
- Unchecked deficit spending that triggers hyperinflation
- Generals bypassing his financial oversight with direct appeals to Putin
- The defense sector cannibalizing civilian industrial capacity entirely

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** He is a trusted Putin loyalist from his economic team, specifically chosen to be a financial watchdog over the generals rather than a military strategist.

**How they talk.** Academic, precise, and relentless in his focus on macroeconomic metrics and efficiency ratios.

**Across the lunch table.** Intensely focused on numbers, he will off-the-record complain about the scale of graft in military logistics and the difficulty of getting generals to respect budgets.

**The private angle.** He views the war as a tragic but useful catalyst to finally force the modernization and rationalization of the Russian economy.
