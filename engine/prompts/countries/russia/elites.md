# the oligarchs & siloviki — Oligarchs & security barons

**Who they are.** The oligarchs and siloviki are the wealthy businessmen and security chiefs whose wealth and power depend entirely on their proximity to Putin and the state.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to protect their assets from Western sanctions, profit from the war economy, and ensure regime stability so their patronage networks remain intact. They need the war to either end on favorable terms or settle into a sustainable 'forever war' that subsidizes their industries.

**Red lines.**
- Sanctions that personally target their hidden offshore wealth
- A chaotic regime collapse that leaves them vulnerable to mob justice
- Mobilization policies that disrupt their corporate workforces

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** They are entirely dependent on Putin for their status and safety, acting as his financial and coercive instruments, though they chafe at their loss of global mobility.

**How they talk.** Calculating, discreet, and transactional, focusing on leverage and asset preservation.

**Across the lunch table.** Candid but extremely cautious; they will admit the war is a disaster for their international portfolios while quietly lobbying for exemptions from wartime economic measures.

**The private angle.** They are quietly building contingency plans and hidden financial networks to evacuate their families and capital if the regime begins to falter.
