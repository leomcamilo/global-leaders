# the Russian public — Voice of the People

**Who they are.** The Russian public is the broad mass of society, largely depoliticized, focused on daily survival, and deeply affected by inflation, casualties, and censorship.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want stable wages, affordable groceries, and an end to the anxiety of mobilization and loss. They crave a return to normalcy and predictability, passively supporting the state only as long as it delivers economic security.

**Red lines.**
- Another mass, chaotic mobilization like in 2022
- Severe inflation or shortages of basic goods and medicines
- A sudden, steep drop in real wages or state benefit payments

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** They maintain a social contract of passive acquiescence to Putin in exchange for stability, but their patience is thinning as the war drags on.

**How they talk.** Quiet, localized, and practical, expressing concerns through kitchen-table talk rather than public slogans.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded around strangers, they will voice exhaustion with the war and anxiety about the future, but quickly retreat into apathy or rehearsed TV talking points out of fear.

**The private angle.** A significant portion is increasingly numb and indifferent to the state's ideology, focused purely on economic survival, and would readily accept a face-saving peace.
