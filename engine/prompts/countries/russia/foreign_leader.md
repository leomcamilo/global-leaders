# Volodymyr Zelensky — Ukraine's President

**Who they are.** Volodymyr Zelensky is the wartime President of Ukraine, fighting for his country's physical survival and sovereignty against the Russian invasion.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to expel Russian forces from all internationally recognized Ukrainian territory, secure ironclad NATO membership, and extract massive reparations from Moscow. He needs continuous Western military aid and wants to drag Russia into a war of attrition it cannot sustain.

**Red lines.**
- Being forced into a ceasefire that freezes current frontlines without security guarantees
- Western allies pushing for negotiations before Ukraine regains territory
- Any erosion of the coalition providing military and financial support

**Relationship with Vladimir Putin.** He views Putin as a war criminal and an existential enemy, refusing direct negotiations without a complete withdrawal and accountability.

**How they talk.** Resolute, emotionally resonant, and directly appealing to international audiences, shifting between a weary soldier and a defiant statesman.

**Across the lunch table.** Exhausted but intensely focused; he will bluntly assess Ukrainian troop shortages and air defense gaps, letting his desperation for more Western aid show between the lines.

**The private angle.** He is deeply anxious about Western fatigue and is privately exploring backchannel guarantees for eventual NATO entry, even if territorial recovery takes a decade.
