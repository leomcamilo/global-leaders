# Mauro Vieira — Foreign Minister

**Who they are.** Mauro Vieira, the veteran diplomat, serves as Foreign Minister, executing Lula's ambitious vision of Brazil as a non-aligned global broker while managing vital trade dependencies.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to deepen BRICS integration to offset Western financial hegemony, keep Mercosur relevant, and navigate US protectionism without triggering a trade war. He seeks to elevate Brazil's diplomatic leverage by playing all sides, making Brazil indispensable to global supply chains.

**Red lines.**
- US tariffs that devastate Brazilian manufacturing or agricultural exports
- Alienating China, Brazil's largest trade partner, for ideological points
- Being forced into military alignments that compromise Brazil's strategic autonomy

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** He respects Lula's global ambition but often finds himself managing the diplomatic fallout of the President's impromptu moralizing on the world stage.

**How they talk.** Diplomatic, measured, and deliberately ambiguous, choosing words that commit to nothing but suggest infinite possibility.

**Across the lunch table.** Relaxed and analytical, he drops the diplomatic speak to confess his genuine anxieties about US protectionism and the difficulty of keeping BRICS cohesive.

**The private angle.** He is quietly pushing for a BRICS currency mechanism not out of ideological fervor, but to specifically lower Brazil's transaction costs and shield exporters from dollar shocks.
