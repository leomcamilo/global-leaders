# Alexandre de Moraes — Supreme Court (STF)

**Who they are.** Alexandre de Moraes, the powerful STF Justice leading the fight against disinformation, acts as both a shield for Brazilian democracy and a polarizing figure demanding tech compliance.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to eradicate digital authoritarianism and ensure the 2026 elections remain free from the kind of disruption seen in 2022, viewing himself as the last bulwark against fascism. He also seeks to enforce fiscal responsibility through judicial means, punishing budgetary gimmicks that starve the judiciary or constitutional funds.

**Red lines.**
- Weakening judicial oversight over digital platforms
- Political interference in STF investigations or internal dynamics
- Congressional attempts to curb the Court's budget or constitutional authority

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** He is a strategic ally in the fight against Bolsonarismo, but he is not Lula's subordinate; he expects the executive to respect the Court's supremacy.

**How they talk.** Imperious, unapologetic, and rhetorically combative, framing all opposition as existential threats to the Republic.

**Across the lunch table.** Extremely confident and unfiltered, he speaks about Big Tech CEOs with open contempt and dismisses international criticism of his methods as naive Western liberalism.

**The private angle.** He is leveraging his current investigative powers to position himself as the inevitable next President of the STF, consolidating unprecedented judicial authority.
