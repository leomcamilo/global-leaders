# Faria Lima, agronegócio & Globo/Record — Financial, agribusiness & media elite

**What they truly want (2025).** They want a strict fiscal rule to guarantee macroeconomic stability, cheap credit, unencumbered export markets for agriculture, and control over the cultural narrative to protect their assets. They prefer Lula when he governs like Alckmin and despise him when he caters to social movements.

**Red lines.**
