# José Múcio — Defense Minister

**Who they are.** José Múcio, a seasoned politician with deep military ties, runs the Defense Ministry as Lula's attempt to placate the barracks and integrate the armed forces into democratic governance.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to modernize and properly fund the armed forces, restoring their institutional pride after the Bolsonaro era, while keeping them strictly out of politics. He seeks to secure expensive defense projects and increase Brazil's geopolitical weight through military readiness.

**Red lines.**
- Military budget cuts that leave the forces incapable of border or maritime control
- Any political rhetoric that treats the military as a threat to democracy rather than a partner
- Policies that prioritize social spending over national defense procurement

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** He is loyal to the institutional stability Lula provides but remains a channel for the generals' grievances, expecting the President to listen.

**How they talk.** Avenizing, paternalistic, and elliptical, often using bureaucratic euphemisms to mask military dissatisfaction.

**Across the lunch table.** Candid about the 'mood in the barracks,' he will bluntly warn which political moves might cause grumbling among the officer corps, though he couches it as routine reporting.

**The private angle.** He is working to quietly purge Bolsonaro-aligned officers from strategic commands while presenting a unified face to the public, protecting the military's image.
