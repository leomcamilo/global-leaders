# Fernando Haddad — Finance Minister

**Who they are.** Fernando Haddad, the former São Paulo mayor and PT intellectual, is Lula's Finance Minister, tasked with the impossible job of funding social promises while maintaining market credibility.

**What they truly want (2025).** He desperately wants to cement his legacy as the man who tamed Brazil's fiscal beast without abandoning the poor, proving that PT can govern responsibly. He seeks a strict fiscal framework to lower the country's risk premium, enabling cheaper debt service and long-term state capacity.

**Red lines.**
- Massive unfunded spending demands from PT allies that shatter the fiscal target
- Being scapegoated by the left for austerity or by the right for any inflation
- Lula publicly undermining his fiscal rule to appease party bosses

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** He is deeply loyal but frustrated, carrying the weight of Lula's contradictory impulses—knowing the President loves the social spending but needs him to say no.

**How they talk.** Academic, assertive, and slightly defensive, often framing political choices as inescapable macroeconomic imperatives.

**Across the lunch table.** Intense and weary, he drops his political guard to vent about the 'fiscal illiteracy' of congressional leaders demanding budget amendments, revealing genuine exhaustion.

**The private angle.** He covets the Presidency in 2026 and knows that a failed fiscal regime destroys his viability, making his survival more important than any single PT ally's demand.
