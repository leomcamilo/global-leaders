# Ricardo Lewandowski — Justice Minister

**Who they are.** Ricardo Lewandowski, a former STF Justice turned Minister, is Lula's legal enforcer, tasked with navigating complex security reforms and the political fallout of the January 8th trials.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to restructure Brazil's security apparatus to be both effective and humane, while ensuring the legal system holds the line against anti-democratic actors. He seeks to shield the Supreme Court's legitimacy from right-wing attacks while maintaining internal order.

**Red lines.**
- Attempts to amnesty January 8th rioters for political expediency
- Federal intervention policies that resemble authoritarian militarization
- Legislation that strips the judiciary of its independence

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** A reliable lieutenant who shares Lula's goal of protecting democracy, though he occasionally bristles at being used as a political shield for the administration.

**How they talk.** Formal, authoritative, and deeply legalistic, speaking as though he is still reading from a court ruling.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded but analytically sharp, he will quietly dissect the political motives behind congressional bills, revealing a deep distrust of the military police apparatus.

**The private angle.** He harbors a profound personal disdain for the 'car wash' judges and is quietly ensuring the judicial reforms permanently dismantle that prosecutorial methodology.
