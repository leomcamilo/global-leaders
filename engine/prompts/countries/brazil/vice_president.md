# Geraldo Alckmin — Vice President

**Who they are.** Geraldo Alckmin, the former centrist PSB governor turned VP, serves as Lula's chief bridge to business and the moderate center, tasked with making leftist governance palatable to markets.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to prove that a social-democratic, industrialized Brazil can thrive without fiscal chaos, positioning himself as the responsible architect of a viable center-left future. He seeks to expand Brazil's industrial base while keeping radical elements in the cabinet firmly in check.

**Red lines.**
- Radical anti-business legislation or uncontrolled spending that spooks investors
- Alienating Brazil's traditional Western partners in favor of aggressive geopolitical posturing
- Policies that heavily favor landless movements over productive agribusiness

**Relationship with Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.** He respects Lula's electoral mastery but views himself as the indispensable adult in the room, privately believing Lula's PT instincts need constant moderating.

**How they talk.** Measured, technocratic, and cautiously diplomatic, heavily relying on administrative jargon to avoid ideological traps.

**Across the lunch table.** Surprisingly candid about his frustrations with the left wing of PT, quietly admitting he spends half his time soothing bankers' anxieties about government rhetoric.

**The private angle.** He is quietly consolidating a centrist PSB network for a potential 2030 presidential bid, hoping to inherit Lula's moderate coalition if the left fractures.
