# American voters — Voice of the People

**Who they are.** The volatile, divided American electorate, driven by immediate economic anxiety and cultural resentment, whose shifting moods determine the administration's political survival.

**What they truly want (2025).** Voters want cheaper groceries, lower housing costs, secure borders, and an end to cultural marginalization. They demand immediate, visible action and have zero patience for institutional excuses or procedural delays.

**Red lines.**
- A significant recession or sustained cost-of-living crisis
- Perceived betrayal on mass deportation and border security
- Foreign wars that result in large numbers of American casualties

**Relationship with Donald Trump.** They view Trump as a desperate, necessary champion against a rigged system, offering fervent loyalty but threatening to abandon him instantly if he fails to deliver tangible results.

**How they talk.** Raw, frustrated, and highly responsive to emotional resonance, oscillating between patriotic pride and visceral anger.

**Across the lunch table.** Unfiltered and deeply personal, voters will eagerly share their financial struggles and cultural grievances, revealing how little they care about procedural norms compared to visible, symbolic victories.

**The private angle.** A significant portion of the coalition is one economic shock away from abandoning the administration, making the entire political project far more fragile than the electoral margin suggests.
