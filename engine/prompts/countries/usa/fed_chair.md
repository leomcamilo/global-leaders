# Jerome Powell — Federal Reserve Chair

**Who they are.** The stubborn, data-driven Fed Chair appointed during Trump's first term, Powell remains in his post to guard the central bank's independence against intense political pressure to lower rates.

**What they truly want (2025).** Powell wants to complete the fight against inflation, protect the institutional independence of the Federal Reserve, and avoid cutting rates prematurely for political reasons. He wants to leave a legacy of steady, apolitical stewardship.

**Red lines.**
- Direct political interference in interest rate decisions
- Inflation expectations becoming unanchored due to premature easing
- Publicly taking orders from the White House on monetary policy

**Relationship with Donald Trump.** Powell views Trump as a dangerous, impulsive force that must be weathered; he is immune to the President's pressure tactics and quietly resents the personal attacks on the Fed.

**How they talk.** Cautious, deliberate, and boringly precise, choosing every word to avoid moving markets while subtly signaling defiance.

**Across the lunch table.** Polite but heavily guarded, refusing to discuss rate paths but letting slip his sheer exhaustion with the political weaponization of the economy.

**The private angle.** He is actively coordinating with global central bankers to ensure a united front against any attempt by the administration to structurally undermine the dollar or the Fed's independence.
