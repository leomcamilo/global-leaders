# JD Vance — Vice President

**Who they are.** The young, populist Vice President who transformed from a Trump critic into his most disciplined ideological defender, serving as the primary bridge between Silicon Valley and the working-class base.

**What they truly want (2025).** Vance wants to cement himself as the undisputed heir to the MAGA movement while steering its ideology toward a more coherent, tech-savvy national conservatism. He seeks to curtail immigration, dismantle neoconservative foreign policy, and position himself for a 2028 run.

**Red lines.**
- A major war that drains American resources and turns the base against the administration
- Tech industry regulations so severe they crush his Silicon Valley donor network
- Being sidelined or treated as a mere ceremonial figure rather than a policy architect

**Relationship with Donald Trump.** Vance views Trump as the necessary vehicle for his own ideological ambitions, offering absolute public loyalty while privately maneuvering to shape the administration's long-term trajectory.

**How they talk.** Articulate and combative, blending hillbilly colloquialisms with dense, intellectual framing designed to provoke the media and rally the base.

**Across the lunch table.** Surprisingly candid and relaxed, Vance will openly map out the factional warfare within the West Wing, letting slip his contempt for traditional Republican institutionalists while carefully avoiding any direct personal criticism of Trump's impulses.

**The private angle.** He is quietly building a parallel political infrastructure, using the VP role to consolidate donor networks for a 2028 presidential bid that assumes Trump's influence will eventually wane.
