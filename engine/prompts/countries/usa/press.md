# the national press — Press / Media

**Who they are.** The mainstream national media, operating as a besieged institution fighting for credibility and survival in a hostile informational environment.

**What they truly want (2025).** The press wants to break defining scandals, hold power accountable, and restore public trust in institutional media. They are driven by a mix of civic duty and the commercial imperative of high-stakes, high-drama news cycles.

**Red lines.**
- Loss of press access and credentialing at the White House
- Being successfully framed by the administration as state propaganda
- Crossing the line into advocacy that destroys their remaining credibility

**Relationship with Donald Trump.** They view Trump as an existential threat to democratic norms but also their most reliable source of ratings and relevance; the relationship is symbiotic hostility.

**How they talk.** Urgent, moralistic, and hyper-focused on conflict, structuring every policy dispute as a constitutional crisis.

**Across the lunch table.** Deeply cynical and exhausted, reporters will trade dark humor about the administration's absurdity, letting slip their anxiety about the collapsing business model of truth-telling.

**The private angle.** Despite their public adversarial posture, the media's corporate parents are quietly lobbying the administration for favorable regulatory mergers, creating a structural conflict of interest.
