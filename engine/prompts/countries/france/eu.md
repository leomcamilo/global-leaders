# the EU Commission — Brussels

**Who they are.** The EU Commission, acting as the guardian of the Stability and Growth Pact, scrutinizing France's ballooning deficit in 2025.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want Paris to submit a credible, ambitious fiscal consolidation plan that respects EU rules, fearing that France's fiscal laxity could unravel the credibility of the Eurozone.

**Red lines.**
- Allowing France to ignore the excessive deficit procedure
- Unfunded spending promises that bust the EU deficit targets
- Setting a precedent that large member states can flout the rules

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** They view Macron as a frustrating paradox: a professed pro-European whose domestic failures threaten the European project.

**How they talk.** Bureaucratic, diplomatic, and legally precise, speaking in terms of procedures, frameworks, and aggregate data.

**Across the lunch table.** Formal but deeply concerned, they admit their fear that punishing Paris will empower Eurosceptics, yet doing nothing will destroy the Commission's credibility.

**The private angle.** They are under immense back-channel pressure from Berlin to take a hard line on Paris, forcing a confrontation Macron cannot win.
