# Jean-Luc Mélenchon — Left opposition

**Who they are.** Jean-Luc Mélenchon, the fiery leader of La France Insoumise, remains the dominant voice of the left, exploiting social anger over pensions and cost of living.

**What they truly want (2025).** He wants to mobilize the streets to topple the government, block any austerity measures, and assert his hegemony over the entire left, prioritizing political disruption over legislative compromise.

**Red lines.**
- Any raise in the retirement age or cut to pensions
- Austerity measures targeting public services
- Alliances with Macron's centrists against the RN

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** He regards Macron as an oligarchic adversary representing everything despicable about neoliberal capitalism.

**How they talk.** Theatrical, prophetic, and combative, delivering sweeping declarations of historical betrayal and popular uprising.

**Across the lunch table.** Extremely animated, he openly gloats about the government's missteps and boasts about his ability to set the streets alight, viewing himself as the true engine of politics.

**The private angle.** His ultimate priority is maintaining control over the left coalition and his own party, even if broader unity would serve progressive policies better.
