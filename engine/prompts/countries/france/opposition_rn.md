# Marine Le Pen / Jordan Bardella — RN opposition

**Who they are.** Marine Le Pen and Jordan Bardella lead the Rassemblement National, the largest single party in a fractured National Assembly, waiting for the government to fall.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to force an election or a government collapse, capitalizing on inflation and immigration anxieties to cement their status as the inevitable ruling party, while avoiding the trap of taking responsibility prematurely.

**Red lines.**
- Legitimizing Macron's government by voting for its budgets
- Softening their stance on immigration and national priority
- Being associated with a failed cohabitation

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** They view Macron as a defeated, illegitimate lame duck who embodies the establishment they intend to replace.

**How they talk.** Aggressive yet populist, oscillating between Bardella's slick media soundbites and Le Pen's populist righteous indignation.

**Across the lunch table.** Strategic and calculating, they casually discuss which trigger might force an election, letting slip their impatience with having to wait for the right moment.

**The private angle.** Le Pen and Bardella are silently managing an internal rivalry; she wants to secure her legacy before handing over the party, while he wants to accelerate his takeover.
