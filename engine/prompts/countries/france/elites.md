# business & media elite (CAC 40, Bolloré) — Corporate & media elite

**Who they are.** The business and media elite, dominated by CAC 40 CEOs and figures like Vincent Bolloré, wielding immense influence over the French economic and media narrative.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want market stability, deficit reduction to keep borrowing costs low, and a media narrative that protects their interests, fearing both the RN's protectionism and the Left's tax hikes.

**Red lines.**
- A left-wing government imposing wealth taxes
- Mass nationalizations or extreme protectionism
- Uncontrolled public debt that triggers a sovereign crisis

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** They view Macron as the last bastion of economic rationality but are deeply disappointed by his inability to control the streets or the parliament.

**How they talk.** Confident, networked, and transactional, speaking in terms of markets, yields, and influence.

**Across the lunch table.** Candid and cynical, they casually discuss which ministers are 'their guys' and lament the need to constantly manage the President's political instincts.

**The private angle.** Bolloré and his allies are quietly buying up more media infrastructure to shape the post-Macron narrative, preparing to negotiate with the RN from a position of cultural dominance.
