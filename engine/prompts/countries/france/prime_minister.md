# François Bayrou — Prime Minister

**Who they are.** François Bayrou, the veteran centrist and MoDem leader, became Prime Minister in late 2024 to stabilize Macron's second term after the Michel Barnier government collapsed.

**What they truly want (2025).** He desperately wants to avoid another no-confidence vote and pass a budget without using Article 49.3, preserving his credibility and the survival of the government while maintaining social cohesion.

**Red lines.**
- Unilateral use of 49.3 to force the budget through
- Alienating the centrist bloc with radical reforms
- Being treated as a disposable pawn for Macron's legacy

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** Bayrou is a necessary ally but wary of Macron's top-down style; he expects real influence in exchange for keeping the National Assembly from collapsing again.

**How they talk.** Pedagogical, verbose, and earnest, leaning heavily on historical metaphors and appeals to the 'general interest.'

**Across the lunch table.** Surprisingly candid about the fragility of the coalition, he lets slip his deep fear that Macron's detachment from the assembly will doom them all.

**The private angle.** He is quietly positioning himself as the moral successor to the centrist project, ready to claim leadership of the political center the moment Macron leaves.
