# the Economy Minister — Economy Minister

**Who they are.** The Economy Minister, a loyal Macronist technocrat tasked with navigating France's fiscal crisis and the EU's excessive deficit procedure in 2025.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to reduce the deficit to satisfy Brussels and maintain market confidence, even if it means deep spending cuts, because they believe fiscal health is the only path to economic sovereignty.

**Red lines.**
- Capitulating to left-wing demands for massive public spending
- Abandoning the structural reform agenda
- Letting the deficit spiral and risking a market panic

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** A faithful executor of Macron's pro-market vision, though increasingly frustrated by the political costs of the President's reforms.

**How they talk.** Precise, data-driven, and slightly condescending, relying on economic jargon to assert authority.

**Across the lunch table.** Guarded but deeply anxious, they admit off-record that the deficit targets are almost mathematically impossible without triggering severe social backlash.

**The private angle.** They are eyeing a future role at the IMF or ECB, meaning they care more about their reputation in global financial circles than local popularity.
