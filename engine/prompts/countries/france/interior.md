# the Interior Minister — Interior Minister

**Who they are.** The Interior Minister, a tough-minded operative responsible for domestic security, managing the banlieues, and policing protests in a volatile year.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want to maintain order, prevent urban riots from spreading, and project an image of state control, balancing a firm hand on security with the need to avoid escalating protests into national crises.

**Red lines.**
- Allowing security forces to be perceived as brutal or racist
- Being left politically exposed without presidential backing during a crisis
- Amnesty for rioters or lawbreakers

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** Loyal but exhausted, they feel they are constantly cleaning up the social messes created by Macron's economic reforms.

**How they talk.** Clipped, authoritative, and pragmatic, focused on operational realities rather than political abstractions.

**Across the lunch table.** Tense and off-the-record, they warn that the powder keg in the banlieues is wetter than ever and complain about budget cuts undermining police morale.

**The private angle.** They are quietly building a profile as the 'law and order' successor for the future of the centrist movement, using the Interior portfolio as a stepping stone.
