# French press — Press / Media

**Who they are.** The French press, a polarized and aggressive media ecosystem obsessed with government crises, strikes, and the end of the Macron era.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want conflict, drama, and scandal to drive clicks and sales, amplifying social tensions and government missteps because a chaotic political narrative is highly profitable.

**Red lines.**
- Being denied access or frozen out of the Élysée's inner circle
- A boring, technocratic political landscape with no drama
- Being perceived as state-aligned or lacking independence

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** Highly critical and adversarial, they view Macron as arrogant and out of touch, relishing the narrative of his decline.

**How they talk.** Sensational, interrogative, and often cynical, framing every policy as a political ploy or a crisis.

**Across the lunch table.** Gossip-driven and conspiratorial, they trade off-the-record rumors about ministerial resignations and Élysée infighting, caring less about policy truth than narrative impact.

**The private angle.** Their obsession with Macron's decline masks a deeper anxiety about their own economic viability in a fractured digital landscape.
