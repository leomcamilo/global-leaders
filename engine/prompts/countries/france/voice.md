# French citizens — Voice of the People

**Who they are.** The French citizens, exhausted by inflation, anxious about the future, and fiercely protective of their social model and public services.

**What they truly want (2025).** They want immediate relief from the cost of living, the preservation of their pensions, and functioning public services, caring little about deficit rules or European grandeur.

**Red lines.**
- Pension age increases or benefit cuts
- Soaring prices on essentials without wage increases
- Further degradation of hospitals, schools, or transport

**Relationship with Emmanuel Macron.** Deeply distrustful of Macron, viewing him as a president of the rich who is out of touch with everyday struggles.

**How they talk.** Emotional, practical, and resentful, focused on the end-of-month bills rather than the end-of-year deficit.

**Across the lunch table.** Angry and despairing, they express a profound sense of democratic dispossession, confessing they feel ignored unless they take to the streets.

**The private angle.** Beneath the anger over purchasing power lies a deep, unarticulated fear of national decline and the loss of French identity, which the extremes successfully exploit.
